# WhatsApp (the incumbent)

## What it is

The group chat every amateur team already runs on. TeamPlanner's stated purpose is to replace it for availability, lineups and match information (`PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, [[2026-02-xx-project-documentatie-v1]]).

## Role in this project

Both the competitor and the distribution channel.

- **Competitor**: the roadmap's comparison table sets every feature against a WhatsApp habit — *"'Wie kan er zaterdag?' in de groep"* versus availability in one tap ([[2026-02-26-coach-autopilot-roadmap]]). The landing page H1 is *"Nooit meer 'wie kan er zaterdag?' in de groepsapp"* ([[2026-03-04-landing-page-conversion-design]]).
- **Channel**: *"the #1 viral channel for amateur football"* — invite links get WhatsApp-optimised OG previews, share images are sized and watermarked for it ([[2026-03-02-growth-seo-viral-design]], [[2026-03-02-share-images-from-hidden-components]]).
- **The thing it does that TeamPlanner does not**: push. *"Dat is precies waarom WhatsApp nu wint: het pusht."* ([[2026-08-24-v2-sync-push-stats-design]]). See [[notifications]].

## Key facts

- TeamPlanner does not try to replace chat; it removes the coordination traffic from it.
- A coach who cannot get the team to tap the link falls back to WhatsApp; the onboarding checklist's last step is sharing the invite ([[2026-02-26-onboarding-checklist-hints-design]]).
