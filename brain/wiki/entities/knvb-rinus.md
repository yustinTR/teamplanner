# Rinus (KNVB training platform)

## What it is

The KNVB's free training-exercise platform for amateur coaches.

## Role in this project

The reference competitor for the exercise library. The roadmap's judgement: Rinus *"bestaat maar is overweldigend en niet geïntegreerd met teammanagement"* ([[2026-02-26-coach-autopilot-roadmap]]). TeamPlanner's answer is a smaller, filtered library tied to the team's level and calendar, and the positioning *"onderscheidend van zowel WhatsApp (geen features) als Rinus (geen teammanagement)"*.

## Key facts

- The library's content strategy names *"KNVB materiaal"* among curated sources; licensing of that material is not discussed anywhere (unsourced). See [[training-content]].
