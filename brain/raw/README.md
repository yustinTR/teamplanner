# raw/

Immutable source material for the `teamplanner` brain. Curated by Yustin;
never edited, renamed or deleted by agents. See `../CLAUDE.md` for the rules.

What belongs here: coach and player feedback, screenshots of real use,
competitor and market notes, analytics exports, KNVB or voetbal.nl findings,
app-store material. One dated subfolder per delivery
(`2026-11-03-feedback-coach-x/`), oldest first.

What does **not** belong here: design documents and implementation plans. Those
live in `../../docs/plans/`, where the `brainstorming` and `writing-plans`
skills write them, and the brain ingests them from there.
